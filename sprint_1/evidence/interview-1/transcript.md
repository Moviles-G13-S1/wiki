**You:** Okay, entonces. First question, tell me a bit about yourself, what do you usually shop for online?

**You:** Okay, okay. So books and clothes. Uh, okay. How often would you say you browse online stores or social media for things you might want to buy?

**Emilio:** More or less once a week.

**You:** Okay, once a week. Mmm... walk me through the last time you saw something online you wanted to buy but didn't buy right away. What did you do next?

**Emilio:** Uh... the last time I wanted to buy something... it was... some pants. Uh, well nothing, I kept looking at them and in the end I decided not to buy them and that's it, I just closed it.

**You:** Okay. Mmm, where do you currently keep track of things you want to buy? Is it notes, an app, screenshots, browser tabs, Instagram, like saved posts?

**Emilio:** Uh, I have... I have... I have an Amazon wishlist for my books. And that's it, on that side.

**You:** And for clothes? Nothing?

**Emilio:** No. Nothing.

**You:** Okay. Eh, have you ever lost track of something you wanted to buy or forgotten about it entirely?

**Emilio:** Yes, many times.

**You:** And what happened? Why did you forget?

**Emilio:** Because, I don't know, well, it wasn't that important.

**You:** Mmm, when you're deciding between similar products, how do you compare them? Regarding price, reviews, options?

**Emilio:** Uh, price.

**You:** Okay. Do you ever go back and check if the price of something changed since you first saw it? How?

**Emilio:** Well, when they pop up in ads... I go back in and usually the same ad pops up ten times. So well, I check it there.

**You:** Have you ever shared a wishlist or product idea with someone else? For, for example I don't know, a gift, a trip, or just an opinion?

**Emilio:** Uh... trips sometimes. I share travel stuff with friends and that's it.

**You:** *(Laughs)* Okay. Mmm... okay. If you could organize everything you want to buy into separate lists, like for example clothes, tech, gifts, travel, books in your case, uh, would that be useful to you? Why or why not?

**Emilio:** Uh... yes, but I surely wouldn't go back to them, I mean, I think it's cool to like check them, but... I don't find it that practical.

**You:** Why not?

**Emilio:** Because... because well I don't know, like you fill that up with a lot of things and in the end you don't do anything.

**You:** Okay. What information would you want to save about an item?

**Emilio:** Mmm... If I can buy it in Colombia or not?

**You:** Mmm... Yeah, sure, why not. Uh, would you want to keep your wishlist private or would you want to share it with your friends and family?

**Emilio:** No, I prefer it to be private.

**You:** Okay. What would you make... uh, what would make you actually use an app like this instead of your current method?

**Emilio:** Mmm... That it takes me directly to the web pages.

**You:** Okay. Uh, is there anything about managing your wants that frustrates you that we haven't talked about?

**Emilio:** Mmm... uh... that I... I don't know, that I get too much information about it, it overwhelms me.

**You:** Okay. Done bro, that was it. You're the best.
