Persona Goals

What does the persona want? (Desires, motivations, etc.)

"I want to know exactly which features keep people using WhyNot, not just guess."
"A notification nobody opens isn't a win — it's noise we need to fix."

Needs & Expectations

Clear event data linking price alerts to purchases
Reliable price-tracking data across all connected stores
One dashboard instead of scattered logs and spreadsheets

Desires & Motivations

Prove which features drive retention and revenue
Guide the team on what to build next
Why?
Because scattered, unreliable event data makes it hard to tell which features are actually working, risking a roadmap guided by guesswork instead of evidence.
Because leadership expects data-backed decisions, and she needs a clear story linking user behavior to retention and revenue before proposing what's next.

Persona Pain Points

What difficulties does the Persona have? What impediments?

Can't tell if a price-drop alert actually led to a purchase
Wishlist saves aren't linked to downstream purchase intent
Engagement data on categories is scattered across sources
No visibility into why users stop checking their wishlists
This makes it hard to prioritize which features to refine in the next sprint.
This risks investing in features that don't actually move retention or revenue.
