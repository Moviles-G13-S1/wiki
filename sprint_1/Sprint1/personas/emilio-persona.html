Persona Goals

What does the persona want? (Desires, motivations, etc.)

"I just want to buy what I need without extra steps or clutter."
"I don't find it that practical to keep going back to lists."

Needs & Expectations

A quick way to check if an item is available in Colombia
A place to keep track of clothes he wants (not just books)
To be taken directly back to the product page

Desires & Motivations

Convenience over organization
Keeping his wishlist private
Why?
Because comparing by price is his main decision factor, and he doesn't want to lose track of an item before he's ready to compare it.
Because he only wants a tool that reduces effort — if it adds more steps than just closing a tab, he won't use it.

Persona Pain Points

What difficulties does the Persona have? What impediments?

Forgets about items he wanted to buy over time
Has no tracking system for clothes, only for books
Feels overwhelmed by too much product information
Only rechecks prices by chance, through repeated ads
This means items he genuinely wanted can slip away simply because "it wasn't that important" in the moment.
This makes price-tracking unreliable and entirely dependent on retargeting ads reappearing.
