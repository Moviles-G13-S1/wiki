Persona

Who is the persona?

Basic info

Carlos, 29
Full-time professional
Practical online shopper

Demographics

Based in Bogota, Colombia
Buys electronics, home products & gifts
Researches purchases carefully before spending
Saves items in tabs, bookmarks & screenshots

Persona Goals

What does the persona want? (Desires, motivations, etc.)

"I want a durable, reliable product at a fair price."
"I wish I could see all my options in one place."

Needs & Expectations

A simple way to compare prices, reviews and specifications
Reliable price-change and availability information
Easy access to warranties, shipping and return policies

Desires & Motivations

Long-term value over the lowest price
Confidence that he made the right decision
Why?
Because Carlos has a limited monthly budget and wants to avoid wasting money on a product that will not last or meet his needs.
Because comparing scattered information takes time, and he wants to make careful decisions without becoming overwhelmed.

Persona Pain Points

What difficulties does the persona have? What impediments?

Too many similar products and scattered comparison details
Conflicting reviews make it hard to judge reliability
Prices change while he waits for a better deal
Saved products become disorganized or are forgotten
This makes research feel slow and exhausting, so Carlos may postpone a useful purchase even after spending considerable time comparing it.
This creates anxiety about missing discounts or stock and makes him repeatedly revisit the same stores, tabs and screenshots.
